What is GhostBundle?

GhostBundle is a small collection of free Windows utilities built for people who want their tools to stay out of the way. Every app is portable, offline-first, and designed to do exactly one thing well. No account, no sign-in, no telemetry — just a folder you can drop anywhere and run.

The bundle currently ships six apps: a clipboard text-to-speech reader, a multi-voice script studio, a hierarchical notepad, a PDF and scan stamper, a text expander, and a calculator that can insert its answer straight into another window.

The apps in detail

Ghost Reader — text-to-speech clipboard reader

Copy any text, and Ghost Reader speaks it aloud. Useful for proofreading, reading long articles hands-free, or listening to notes on a second monitor. You can export a session to MP3 and resume where you left off.

Ghost Cast — multi-voice script studio

Write a script, assign a different voice to each character, and add sound effects or background music. Ghost Cast renders the whole thing to a single MP3 — handy for audio dramas, tabletop RPG intros, or narrated slideshows.

Ghost Notes — hierarchical portable notepad

Notes live in one file you can carry on a USB stick or sync yourself. Rich text, nested pages, automatic backups, and a search that looks across every note at once.

GhostStamp Pro — PDF and image stamper

Apply clean vector seals to PDFs and scanned images. Sixteen preset seals cover the usual cases — Approved, Paid, Draft, Confidential, and so on — and you can export the result as a stamped PDF or a PNG. Everything runs offline.

Ghost Phrases — text expander

Define a short trigger, and Ghost Phrases types the full paragraph for you wherever your cursor happens to be. Boilerplate replies, signatures, and snippets stop being a copy-paste chore.

Ghost Calc — insert-anywhere calculator

A standard keypad with memory and percent, plus five worksheets: mortgage, fixed deposit, recurring saver, unit conversion, and date calculation. The difference is that Ghost Calc can type its result directly into whichever window is focused — no manual copy-paste.

Frequently asked questions

Is GhostBundle really free?

Yes. Every app is free forever. There are no ads, no upsells, no account required, and no telemetry of any kind.

Do the apps need an internet connection?

Mostly no — but it depends on the voice you choose. All six apps run entirely on your machine: no accounts, no sign-ins, no cloud sync, and no telemetry. Download them once and use them forever offline.

The one exception is online voices. If you pick a cloud-backed SAPI voice such as Microsoft Xiaoxiao Online or Microsoft Yunxi Online, the app needs an active internet connection while it speaks, because the audio is synthesized on Microsoft's servers rather than on your PC. Local Desktop voices (David, Zira, Hazel) work fully offline, forever.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 7 SP1 or later, with .NET Framework 4.x installed. The apps are portable — no installer required, and no registry changes.

Where can I get help with a specific app?

Each app has its own help page. Use the navigation bar at the top of this page, or click the Docs button on any app card.

Can I redistribute or bundle these apps?

The full license terms ship with each application. In short, they are free to use, but please read the license included in each download for details.
